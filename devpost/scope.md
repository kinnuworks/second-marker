# Second Marker

One line: after board results, it reads the scanned copy of your marked answer book against the board's official marking scheme and tells you which questions are worth paying to re-evaluate, and which to leave alone.

## The Unique Kernel
It finds the marks the examiner missed, step by step against the official marking scheme, and turns them into a money-and-risk decision: "dispute these 3 questions, skip the rest." Re-evaluation costs money per question, is capped at 10 questions, and can lower your marks, so knowing *which* questions is the whole problem.

## Who It's For
A Class 12 CBSE student in the week after results. They've paid for the scanned copy of their marked Maths answer book, the re-evaluation window is a few days long, and the marks feel wrong. Today they squint at 20 pages alone, or wait for a teacher who may not have time before the window closes. Exam-prep guides tell them to get the photocopy checked by a teacher or expert before applying.

## The Core Loop
Open it → load the answer book (or the sample) → watch it re-add every margin mark and line each answer up against the scheme → see a ledger of questions marked **Dispute** or **Skip**, with fee, likely gain and the risk of losing marks → confirm the picks → print a filing sheet.
It's a once-a-season tool, used hard for a few days; students come back for each subject.

## Inspiration & Identity
The answer book itself. Ruled answer-book paper, the examiner's red ink, and a second ink for the "second marker" (the re-evaluator's findings). Rubber-stamp verdicts (DISPUTE / SKIP) borrowed from Indian exam-office paperwork. Plain, calm, honest; never "AI-powered" hype. Avoid the warm-beige-and-serif look many entries already use.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
No personal story. The learner chose this because it scored highest on usefulness and novelty in the research, and no other entry in the competition does it. The case rests on sourced facts:
- CBSE re-evaluation: scanned copy first, then verification, then per-question re-evaluation; marks can go up or down. https://www.pw.live/news/cbse-class-12-re-evaluation-2026
- 28,000+ re-evaluation/verification requests in one year. https://news.careers360.com/cbse-class-12-revaluation-verification-results-2025-out-at-results-cbse-nic-in/amp
- Max 10 questions per subject; guides advise getting the photocopy checked by a teacher first. https://vidyaprakashan.com/cbse-re-evaluation-2025/

## What "Working" Looks Like
A judge clicks **Try the sample answer book**. Within seconds they see a real-looking marked Maths answer book, a red flag that the examiner's total doesn't add up, and Question 27 opened side by side: the student's working on the left, the official scheme's steps on the right, one step ticked "written, not credited: +1 mark." The ledger stamps three questions DISPUTE and the rest SKIP, with ₹300 total and the risk spelled out. One click prints the filing sheet.
The "oh, that's cool" beat: the stamp landing on a question the student would never have spotted.

## The POC Boundary
- One paper: CBSE Class 12 Mathematics, 2025–26 official sample paper, with its official marking scheme.
- Read a marked answer book (photos or PDF), re-add the margin marks, compare answers with the scheme, rank dispute/skip with fee and risk, confirm, print a filing sheet.
- A sample answer book that loads in one click. No accounts.

## Later
- Other subjects and the real board papers (schemes rebuilt from published question papers, clearly labelled).
- Class 10, state boards, university revaluation.
- Hindi interface.
- "Is this even my answer book?" handwriting check.

## Explicitly Cut
- Filing the application for the student: the board's portal does that, and it needs the student's login.
- Accounts and saved history: not needed to prove the kernel.
- Predicting the re-evaluated score as a promise: it gives evidence and odds, never a guarantee.
