Let r = radius of the ice ball at time t.
V = (4/3) π r3 ...(1) S = 4 π r2 ...(2)
dV/dt ∝ S ⇒ dV/dt = −k S , k > 0 ...(3)
Differentiating (1) w.r.t. t :
dV/dt = (4/3) π · 3r2 · dr/dt = 4 π r2 dr/dt ...(4)
From (3) and (4) : − k S = 4 π r2 dr/dt
− k S = S · dr/dt (using (2))
⇒ dr/dt = − k
∴ the radius decreases at a constant rate k.
